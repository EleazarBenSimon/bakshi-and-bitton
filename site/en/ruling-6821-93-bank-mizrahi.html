Declarative

CA 6821/93

United Mizrahi Bank Ltd. v. Migdal Cooperative Village

In the ruling that launched the "constitutional revolution," an expanded panel of nine unelected justices claimed for itself the power to strike down the Knesset's primary legislation — in a case where it actually upheld the Family Agricultural Sector (Arrangements) Law, dismissing the banks' appeal unanimously. President Barak placed the Basic Laws above the Knesset and built the "constituent authority" theory; Justice Cheshin contested the very basis for that supremacy. A new power was proclaimed without yet being used. P"D 49(4) 221.

9 justicesVote 9–0Lead opinion: Aharon Barak, Shlomo Levin, Mishael Cheshin, Theodor Or, Eliezer Goldberg, Gavriel Bach, Yaakov Maltz, Tzvi Tal, Dalia Dorner
Why this matters
This is where the power was born. The Court conjured the authority to strike down Knesset laws out of thin air — in a case where it struck down nothing, so there was no losing party able to appeal the innovation itself. The claim slipped in quietly, and every later annulment — up to the voiding of a Basic Law amendment in 2023 — draws its authority from here. A supremacy the Court proclaimed for itself, over the elected legislature, without the legislature ever granting it.
