AAA 662/11

Sela et al. v. Head of Kfar Vradim Local Council Sivan Yehieli

Three unelected justices (Hayut, Hendel, Vogelman) unanimously reversed the district court and told an elected local council what to build: in AAA 662/11 (Sept. 9, 2014, lead opinion Vogelman) they branded the Kfar Vradim Council's decision not to build a mikveh "unreasonable," voided it, and ordered the Council to proceed with construction. Using the elastic reasonableness test, the Court substituted its own budgetary judgment for that of the elected body. The Council complied.

3 justicesVote 3–0Lead opinion: Uzi Vogelman

AAA 662/11 is a ruling of the Supreme Court of Israel, decided 2014-09-09, by a panel of 3 justices. Outcome: Struck down. Vote: 3–0.

Why this matters
Here "reasonableness" descended from cabinet ministers to the budget ledger of an elected local council. The Kfar Vradim Council, chosen by its own residents, decided not to build a mikveh — a question of priorities and money; three unelected justices branded that decision "unreasonable" and told the council what to build. It is another turn of the same elastic instrument: not a test of the act's legality but a substitution of the judges' judgment for the elected body's — and at the very level where voters sit closest to those they elect. The council complied, adding one more precedent to the corpus in which "reasonableness" steers the decisions of those the public empowered, rather than the Court.

Notes

Justice Vogelman wrote the lead opinion. Borderline-scope entry: this is a local-authority decision rather than a ministerial or cabinet decision. Included because it is uniformly cited in the IDI/INSS/Cohen-Eliya literature as part of the reasonableness-review corpus and because Israeli local government, like national government, is elected. Heard as an administrative appeal (עע"ם) by the Supreme Court.
