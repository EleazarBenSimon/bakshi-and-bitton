Mandatory order

HCJ 5819/24

Movement for Quality (Abolition of) Government in Israel v. Minister of Defense et al.

Five unelected justices seized the most explosive political decision in the country: on 19 November 2025 an expanded panel led by Deputy President Noam Sohlberg issued a unanimous order absolute dictating that the elected government formulate, within 45 days (by 4 January 2026), an enforcement policy to actually conscript Haredi yeshiva students — the Court compelling the executive not merely to legislate but to enforce.

2 justicesLead opinion: Noam Sohlberg

HCJ 5819/24 is a ruling of the Supreme Court of Israel, decided 2025-11-19, by a panel of 2 justices. Outcome: Mandatory order.

Why this matters
Here the power crossed from annulment to command. Until now the Court had struck down government acts or removed officials from office; now, in a unanimous order absolute by an expanded panel led by Deputy President Noam Sohlberg, it went beyond holding that the exemption had no lawful basis — it dictated to the elected executive a 45-day timetable to formulate an enforcement policy and to conscript in practice. The Court thereby seized not only the authority to tell the government what it may not do, but the authority to order how it must act and at what pace — a question of prioritization and enforcement that lies at the core of the political judgment entrusted to the elected branch. When the government missed the deadline, the matter slid into contempt proceedings, and a justice from the panel, David Mintz, declared that the rule of law had collapsed — a measure of how far an unelected Court had reached into executive discretion.

Decision under review
The government's failure to formulate and apply an effective enforcement policy for conscripting Haredi yeshiva students after the blanket exemption lapsed, and the lack of actual enforcement of draft orders that had been issued and ignored.
Outcome

Compliance status
contested
Defiance signals
The government did not meet the 45-day deadline (4 January 2026). The Movement for Quality (Abolition of) Government filed a contempt-of-court motion; at a subsequent hearing Justice David Mintz stated that the rule of law had failed. Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara publicly stated that the government was acting in violation of the ruling. As of the latest verified reporting the enforcement and contempt proceedings remained ongoing; no final contempt finding has been confirmed.
