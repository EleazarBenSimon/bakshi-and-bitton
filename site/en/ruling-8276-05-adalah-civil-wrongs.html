HCJ 8276/05

Adalah — The Legal Center for Arab Minority Rights in Israel et al. v. Minister of Defense

Nine unelected justices, led by President Barak, unanimously voided an act of the Knesset: Amendment No. 7 to the Civil Wrongs (Liability of the State) Law, which had exempted the state from liability for civil damage in 'conflict zones'. The people's representatives drew the line on where the state opens its wallet; the Court ruled they could not, holding that the blanket exemption disproportionately violated the rights to life, dignity, and property under Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty.

9 justicesVote 9–0Lead opinion: Aharon Barak
Why this matters
Here the power born in 1993 is cashed in full against an ordinary statute. The Knesset made a quintessentially budgetary and political choice — where the state bears its financial liability for damage caused in a "conflict zone" — and nine unelected justices voided it unanimously, 9-0, under the hand of President Barak. The ground for annulment lay not in any explicit text of a Basic Law but in the "proportionality" the Court itself developed under Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty. The decision over where the public purse carries liability was taken from the voters' representatives to the justices' table.
