HCJ 8948/22

Movement for Quality (Abolition of) Government in Israel et al. v. Prime Minister et al.

Eleven unelected justices, 10-1, overrode the elected Prime Minister's discretion and ejected a lawfully appointed minister — Shas chairman Aryeh Deri — invoking the "reasonableness" doctrine the Court itself fashioned. True, Deri carried a tax-fraud conviction and had told a court under a plea bargain that he would retire from public life; but the Court seized for itself the call of who may sit as a minister, taking it from the voters and their representatives. Justice Elron alone dissented.

11 justicesVote 10–1Lead opinion: Esther Hayut

HCJ 8948/22 is a ruling of the Supreme Court of Israel, decided 2023-01-18, by a panel of 11 justices. Outcome: Struck down. Vote: 10–1.

Why this matters
Here the power reached past laws to people. No statute was struck — a man the voters' representatives had appointed was removed from the government, in the name of "reasonableness," a ground the Court itself fashioned and no one legislated. Eleven unelected justices decided who is fit to sit at the cabinet table — a call taken from the voters and their representatives.
