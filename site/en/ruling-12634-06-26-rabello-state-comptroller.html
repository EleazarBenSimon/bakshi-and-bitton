HCJ 12634-06-26

Yehuda Ressler et al. v. The Knesset et al. (consolidated petitions)

The High Court of Justice unanimously ruled, in a five-justice panel led by Deputy President Sohlberg, that the Knesset's election (3 June 2026) of attorney Michael Rabello as State Comptroller was invalid due to a fundamental breach of ballot secrecy after Knesset members photographed their ballots. The Court ordered a new election to be held.

5 justicesVote 5–0Lead opinion: Noam Sohlberg

The Knesset plenum's election (3 June 2026) of attorney Michael Rabello — Prime Minister Netanyahu's personal lawyer — as State Comptroller, in a second round of voting he won 61-57 against retired Justice Yosef Elron, after Knesset Speaker Amir Ohana announced that the vote could be photographed and coalition members photographed their ballots or left them exposed outside the voting booth, allegedly violating the legally mandated secrecy of the ballot.
