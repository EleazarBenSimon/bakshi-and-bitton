How are these figures produced?

Every figure on this page is computed at build time from rulings.json and corpus_stats.json, the same records the site publishes. No figure is stored or typed by hand, so any number here can be recomputed from the published data. Each case links to the official court file. Percentages are rounded to whole numbers and may not sum to 100.
