HCJ 3132/15

Yesh Atid Party v. Prime Minister

Unanimously (5–0), led by President Naor, the Court voided the 'deputy-minister-as-minister' arrangement that PM Netanyahu devised to put Yaakov Litzman atop the Health Ministry with no appointed minister. Though each appointment was lawful on its own, the justices struck the combination as 'unreasonable' — the elastic doctrine by which they dictated to the elected government how it may staff itself, ordering the arrangement dismantled within 60 days.

5 justicesVote 5–0Lead opinion: Miriam Naor
Why this matters
Here reasonableness reached past the single appointment to the architecture of the government itself. The justices conceded that each appointment was lawful on its own — yet unanimously voided the combination the elected leadership had chosen, by force of the "reasonableness" ground the Court fashioned and no legislature enacted. The coalition's discretion over how to assemble a cabinet and divide its portfolios was thereby subordinated to the review of five unelected justices, who even ordered the arrangement dismantled within 60 days. The call over who runs a ministry, and how, was taken from the Prime Minister and the voters' representatives and lodged with the Court.
