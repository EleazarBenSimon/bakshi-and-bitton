HCJ 18225-06-25

Gillon et al. v. Government of Israel et al. (petitions against Cabinet Resolutions 3125 and 3306)

For the first time in Israeli history the elected cabinet voted — unanimously — to fire its own Attorney General, Gali Baharav-Miara. An expanded panel of seven unelected justices, led by President Amit, unanimously voided that decision (Resolution 3306) and the amendment that preceded it (Resolution 3125, which had removed the duty to consult the public-professional committee), on procedural grounds: haste, thin record, no consultation. The Court forced the cabinet's own legal gatekeeper to stay in office — entrenching her post against the elected government short of primary legislation.

7 justicesVote 7–0Lead opinion: Yitzhak Amit
Why this matters
If in the Deri case the Court decided who may sit at the cabinet table, here it decided who controls the cabinet's own gatekeeper. For the first time the elected government voted, unanimously, to end the Attorney General's tenure — and seven unelected justices, unanimously, forced her back into office without striking a single statute, scrutinizing only how the decision was made. The mechanism for ending that tenure was thereby placed beyond the reach of the entire government, and the choice of who serves as Attorney General passed from the voters and their representatives to the Court — which set its own gatekeeper above those meant to appoint her.

Defiance signals
Several cabinet ministers publicly criticized the ruling and called for non-compliance, as reported in mainstream Israeli press (see secondary_urls). No formal cabinet act of defiance was taken. The ruling is now the leading authority cited against any future attempt to alter the AG-termination mechanism by cabinet resolution rather than primary legislation.

Notes

First time in Israeli history a cabinet voted to fire an AG. The High Court immediately froze the dismissal in August 2025; the December 2025 ruling made the freeze permanent and voided both underlying resolutions. President Amit authored the lead opinion.
