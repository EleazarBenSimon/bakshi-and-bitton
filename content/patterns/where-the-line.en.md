# Where the Right Draws the Line

This page collects attributed statements by figures on the political right regarding the government's July 2026 decision not to comply with a High Court ruling — **in their own words**. Quotation only, with no interpretation added by the project. Each statement carries its source and a confidence level: **Verified** (an on-record public statement, from a primary source or several) versus **Reported** (a single-source report, or a private remark). Attributed is not the same as public — that distinction is marked explicitly on every row.
